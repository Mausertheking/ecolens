How it works

  • The answer is structured data (category, bin, prep steps, harm) in English and Azerbaijani, tuned for Azerbaijan's still-developing collection system.
  • Numbers exclude demo and test accounts. Accuracy is what users report; it isn't an expert audit.
  • Photos aren't stored. Only the item name, category and the user's rating are saved.
