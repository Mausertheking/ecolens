Common questions

How many scans can I do?
Every account gets 3 free scans per day. The count resets at midnight UTC.
The answer was wrong. What should I do?
Tap 👎 under the result and type what the item really is. That's how we measure and improve accuracy. A closer, well-lit photo of one item usually helps.
The map shows no recycling points.
Points come from OpenStreetMap, which is still incomplete outside Baku, and its servers are sometimes busy. Try again in a few minutes. Location permission is needed.
I didn't get the confirmation email.
Check your spam folder, then try signing up again with the same email.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: History → Delete my account. Your account and scan history are deleted immediately and permanently.
